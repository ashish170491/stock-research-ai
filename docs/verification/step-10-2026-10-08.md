## Observations (outside scope, not affecting verdict)
- The llm suite is slow (~58 minutes for 24 cases on this machine, qwen3:8b with `numCtx=16384`). This is expected per the roadmap ("takes several minutes") but is worth knowing before re-running it interactively; Maven's own test timeout is not the limiter, but any CI job wrapping it needs a budget well over an hour.
- Several of the 10 failing cases fail on `relevant=false` (the RelevancyEvaluator judge) rather than on intent/tool/fact checks, which all passed for those same cases - e.g. `quote-hdfcbank` and `not-stock-joke` got the right intent and the right facts but the judge model scored the answer as not relevant to the question. This is plausibly judge noise from a small local model (GoldenSetTest's own Javadoc, line 35, anticipates this: "pass rate ... not a strict gate"), not a defect in Step 10's harness, but it is worth tracking across future baseline runs to see if it is a systematic pattern (e.g. the judge penalising short factual answers) rather than noise.
- Two cases (`history-hdfcbank`, `history-techm`, `shareholding-techm`) failed on a genuine missing required fact (e.g. the answer didn't echo the literal symbol or "not available"). These are golden-set calibration items (the fixture/case author's wording vs. the model's actual phrasing), not Step 10 infrastructure bugs - outside this step's criteria, which are about the harness existing and running, not about reaching a particular pass rate.
- `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `pom.xml`, `application.yml` are modified in the working tree alongside the new `golden/` test package; none of the diffs were read in detail since they are consistent with Step 10's own described changes (actuator dependency, golden-set docs, Micrometer config) and no other roadmap step's spec references them.
