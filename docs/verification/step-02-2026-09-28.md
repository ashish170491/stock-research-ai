## Learning check
1. Why does a DATA_CONFLICT P/E produce no earnings yield rather than one calculated from price / EPS? `ValuationService.earningsYield` (:128-135) calculates only from a VALID P/E, and `crossChecked` deliberately omits the implied figure (:109-111). Choosing price / EPS would mean the application silently picks a side in a conflict, which INV-7 forbids. It would also give the model an unverified number to quote as "the P/E".
2. How does the model's P/E figure get checked against evidence at runtime, and where is that evidence captured? `getValuation` calls `ToolUsage.record(context, new ToolUsage.Call(..., text))` (`StockResearchTools.java:193-196`) with the exact rendered text it returns. `AiService` then runs `NumericClaimVerifier.verify(answer, evidence)` followed by `UnsupportedClaimFilter.filter` (:250-251) against that recorded evidence. A figure the tool never returned would be removed and noted.
3. Why is OCF/PAT a ratio of sums over consecutive years rather than an average of yearly ratios, and what happens with four years for a 5y window? `FinancialMetricsService.ocfToPat` (:483-530) uses `FinancialCalculator.sumRatio`, so one year with tiny profit cannot dominate the result. It needs exactly `years` consecutive VALID years. With fewer, it returns UNAVAILABLE with "needs 5 fiscal years ... 4 available" (FinancialMetricsServiceTest:430-439) rather than a partial ratio labelled as 5y.
